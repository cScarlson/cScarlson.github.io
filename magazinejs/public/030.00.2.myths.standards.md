
Myths About Standards
================================================================
Here we elucidate some of what we see as myths around aspects of standardization. Again, feel welcome to dive straight into the catalog of articles that dive deeper and refer back to this article for extra context.



## Styleguides

### Myths
- _A Styleguide needs to tell developers what they should never do_.
- _A Styleguide needs to tell developers what they should always do_.

### Reason
Styleguides that are too strict diminish productivity while those too loose do not guide style enough.



## Defensive Coding

### Myth
_Typical styleguides, linting, and formatters will eliminate Code Rot by themselves_.

### Reason
The most important aspects of corrupted code are resolved by writing Defensive Code. Defensive Coding Practices can feasibly even _replace_ the need for much of the toolchain above.



## Formatting

### Myths
- _Code Formatters are good for everyone._
- _PrettierJS lives up to its claims._
- _There are many legitimate reasons to use PrettierJS._

### Reason
An MIT study in 2012 analyzed over 60 code repositories and concluded that the 2 biggest factors in Code Readability are _File-Length_ and _Indentation_. PrettierJS, virtually the only code formatter used, completely destroys any sense of improving these metrics, indiscriminately, in a highly opinionated and inflexible manner. It also makes developers minds lazier in thinking about syntax while robbing Junior developers the opportunity to learn how to read other styles of coding as they improve their own.



## Bootcamps

### Myths
- _Opening tags in HTML are supposed to be broken out over multiple lines_.
- _Bootcamps never lied to you_.

### Reason
Bootcamps told you "6-figures in 6-weeks" and never corrected you on how you wrote code or how they did.


## AI & LLMs

### Myths

- _AI is safe_

### Reason
It's almost all a lie.
